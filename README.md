# CS-255-System-Analysis and Design
Coursework for CS 255 System Analysis and Design

## About the DriverPass Project
DriverPass was the client for this project. They wanted a web-based system that would help students prepare for their driving tests. The system needed to let customers create accounts, choose training packages, schedule driving lessons, take practice tests, and check their progress. It also needed to help employees manage appointments, customer information, lesson notes, reports, and other daily work.

## What I Did Well
I think I did a good job understanding what the client wanted and turning those needs into system requirements. I also created different diagrams to show how customers and employees would use the system and how different parts of the system work together. These diagrams helped me organize the design and make the system easier to understand.

## What I Would Change
If I could revise one part of my work, I would add more detail to some of the technical requirements in the business requirements document. I would be more specific about system speed, security, and the devices and operating systems the system should support. Adding these details would make the requirements clearer for the development team.

## How I Used the Client’s Needs
I used the DriverPass interview and project information to understand what customers and employees needed from the system. I included features such as account registration, training packages, appointment scheduling, practice tests, lesson notes, reports, and different levels of access. It is important to consider user needs because the system should make their work easier and solve the problems they are actually facing.

## How I Approach System Design
When I design a system, I first try to understand what the client needs and who will be using the system. Then I organize the requirements and use diagrams to show how the system should work. In the future, I would continue using use case, activity, sequence, and class diagrams because they help break a large system into smaller and easier-to-understand parts. I would also review the design with users and make changes based on their feedback.
